# Brief

Written 2026-11-16, before building. The fuller design note is in the
`freelance-portfolio` repo at
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-freelance-section-design.md`.

**Who it is for.** Owners of small businesses, and other people who are not
developers, deciding whether to hire Marlon. They arrive from a message he
sent, a profile link or a referral.

**The one thing it must get them to do.** Send a project brief by email.

**What it must do.**

- Say what Marlon builds in the first screen.
- Show his projects as proof, each labelled as his own, a concept or client
  work. He has no paid clients yet, and the site must never suggest otherwise.
- Explain how a job runs and answer the first questions a buyer has.
- Have a slot for client work that stays hidden until a real one exists.
- Keep old links working: `/resume` and `/projects` go to the job site.

**Out of scope.** Prices, a contact form with a server, a blog, analytics,
testimonials.
